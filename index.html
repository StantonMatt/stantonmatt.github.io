Personal MCP Gateway

Personal MCP Gateway is my private, self-hosted instance of Executor, open-source software licensed under the MIT License. It runs on my own computer, bound to localhost, and is not offered to the public.

I am its only user. It uses Google OAuth to let my local AI agents, including Claude Code and Codex, read, search, draft and manage email and basic Gmail settings for my own Google accounts, on my behalf.
